✦ QUICK START

How to set it up
in five little steps.

Takes about two minutes. The only part people miss is step 2 — so start there if your friend has gone quiet.

▲☁⌘↑ it lives up here
  1. 1
    Download it for your device.

    Grab the Windows .exe or the macOS .dmg — whichever one matches your computer — and install it like any other app.

  2. 2
    Now look at your tray.

    Heads up: Choto You has no window to open. There is no icon in your Dock or Start menu. It lives in the menu bar on macOS and the system tray on Windows — click the little face up there and everything opens from that menu.

  3. 3
    Copy the prompt from Characters.

    Open Characters from that tray menu. There is a ready-made prompt waiting for you — copy it.

  4. 4
    Paste it into any AI chat.

    ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — any of them works. Paste the prompt and it hands you back a PNG sprite sheet of your character.

  5. 5
    Upload the PNG, and that’s it.

    Drop the image back into Characters and your new friend walks onto your desktop. From there you can set reminders and alarms whenever you like.

No account, no sign-up, nothing leaves your computer. ♡

Is Choto You free?+

Yes, the app is available to download for free from its official GitHub Releases page.

Does it work on both Windows and Mac?+

Yep! There are builds for Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and a universal .dmg for both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

Can I add my own character?+

Yes. Bring an animated sprite sheet or a folder of pictures, and customize the tiny character who lives on your desktop.

Does it collect any of my data?+

No. The desktop app stores its data locally, without accounts, cloud services, telemetry, or an automatic update check.

Why does my computer show an installer warning?+

Current builds are unsigned. macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen can display a first-launch warning. Review the release details and source code before installing.
